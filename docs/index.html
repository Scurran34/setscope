setscope

A personal project for analyzing DJ sets. Work in progress.

setscope looks at DJ tracklists from 1001Tracklists and answers questions like: which DJs play a song the most, which unreleased IDs keep showing up next to it, what it's usually played with, and whether an artist is being played more lately. It runs locally on my own computer.

What it does

  • Song pages: top DJs, recurring IDs, songs played alongside, every set with a SoundCloud listen link, genre, key and BPM.
  • Artist pages: profile, their sets, their most played songs, who they play, and whether other DJs are playing them more.
  • Trending: songs and artists whose share of sets is rising.
  • Likes and categories: sets that play your liked songs, and songs that might fit your categories.

How it's built

TypeScript on Node, with the data-access layer kept separate from pure analysis functions that are covered by unit tests. Collecting is deliberately polite: an honest user agent, at least 8 seconds between requests, local caching so nothing is fetched twice, and it stops on any sign of a block.
